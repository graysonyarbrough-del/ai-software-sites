1. What you get

Zorlo builds, hosts and looks after a website for Cloud Chaser for as long as you subscribe.

  • Your website: a mobile-friendly site covering both locations, with addresses, hours, phone numbers, products and a contact form.
  • Website requests: messages from the site's contact form are sent to you by email or text, whichever you choose.
  • Hosting: we keep the site online. Your own web address is connected at no extra charge if you already own it. If you don't, we can register one for you at cost.
  • Updates: up to 2 small changes a month (hours, phone numbers, prices, photos, products added or removed), usually done within 3 business days.
  • Support: by email or phone during normal business hours.

Not included unless agreed in writing: online ordering or payments, logos, professional photos, ads or paid marketing.

2. Price and billing

  • Setup fee: $2,000, one time, due when this agreement is signed. It covers designing and building the website. Work starts once it is paid.
  • Monthly fee: $79 per month, starting the day the website goes live. It covers hosting, updates and support.
  • Billing: the monthly fee is charged automatically to the card or bank account you give us, on the same day each month.
  • Price changes: we'll give you at least 30 days' notice by email before any change to the monthly fee. You can cancel before it takes effect.
  • Late or failed payments: if a payment fails and isn't fixed within 14 days, we may take the site offline until it's paid.
  • Refunds: the setup fee is not refundable once the website has gone live.

3. What we need from you

  • Accurate details: business name, addresses, phone numbers, email, hours and products. We put on your site only what you give us or approve.
  • Your content: any photos, logos or text you send us must be yours or something you have permission to use.
  • Compliance: you confirm that the products and claims shown on the site follow the laws that apply to tobacco and vape sellers, including age rules.
  • Approval: you look over the site before it goes live and tell us about anything wrong.
  • Your web address: if you own your domain, you keep it in your name and pay its yearly renewal, unless we've agreed to manage it.

4. Cancelling, ownership and limits

  • Cancel anytime: email or call us. No cancellation fee. You won't be charged again, and the site stays online until the end of the month you've paid for.
  • Your content stays yours: your business name, logo, photos and text belong to you. When you cancel, we'll send you a copy of your website's files if you ask within 30 days.
  • Your leads: contact-form messages go to you. We don't sell or share your customers' information.
  • Uptime: we work to keep your site online but can't promise it will never go down, for example during hosting outages.
  • Limit of responsibility: if something goes wrong on our side, the most Zorlo owes you is what you paid us in the last 3 months.
  • Governing law: the laws of the State of Georgia govern this agreement.
  • Changes: changes to this agreement must be agreed in writing; email counts.

5. Optional add-on: Local SEO package

This add-on helps both Cloud Chaser shops show up higher on Google Search, Google Maps and AI tools like ChatGPT. It is optional and only applies if you choose "Yes" when signing.

Price: $400 per month, starting the month you sign up. The first 3 months are required, because SEO takes time to show results. After that it is month to month.

Setup (first 2 weeks)

  • Update both Google Business Profiles: hours, categories, photos, website links and common questions
  • Set up Bing Places and Apple Business Connect for both shops
  • List both shops on Yelp, Facebook and other directories with matching name, address and phone
  • Set up Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and website tracking
  • Make review QR codes for both counters
  • Record where the shops rank today, so progress can be measured

Every month

  • 4 Google posts per shop (new products, deals, news)
  • Reply to every new Google review
  • Keep the website's products, prices and deals up to date
  • Add new photos to both Google profiles
  • Check rankings on Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity
  • Send a short monthly report: calls, direction requests, website visits, reviews and rankings

What you agree to

  • Add Zorlo as a manager (not owner) on both Google Business Profiles. You keep full ownership.
  • Keep the review QR codes at both counters and ask customers for reviews
  • Send new products, deals and photos when you have them

Not included

  • Paid ads of any kind
  • Fake or paid reviews. Zorlo will never post these.
  • Health claims about any product. All posts follow 21+ and product rules.

No ranking guarantee. Google and AI tools decide their own results, so no one can promise a #1 spot. Zorlo promises to do the work above every month and report results honestly.

Canceling the add-on: after the first 3 months, cancel any time with 30 days' written notice. Canceling the SEO add-on does not cancel the website plan. You keep everything set up in your name, including Google profiles, listings and reviews.
