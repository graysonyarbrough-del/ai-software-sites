Six layers between your attic and the next hailstorm.

  1. Roof deckingSolid OSB sheathing. Soft or rotted sheets get replaced, not covered.
  2. Ice and water shieldSelf-sealing membrane at the eaves and valleys, where water backs up first.
  3. Synthetic underlaymentA tear-resistant second barrier across the whole deck.
  4. Drip edge and starter stripMetal edging sends water into the gutters. Starter shingles seal the first row.
  5. Architectural shinglesThick laminated shingles rated for high winds, hand-nailed six per shingle.
  6. Ridge vent and capLets hot attic air out along the peak, then capped to match the roof.

The part you never see is the part that counts.

The layers under your shingles are what actually keep water out of your attic. We don't skip any of them, and you can ask to see each one before it's covered.

Questions we get a lot

Do I need to be home for the inspection?

Not for the roof itself. If you'd like us to check the attic or walk you through what we found, plan to be there.

Do you work on metal roofs?

Yes. We install and repair metal roofing as well as shingle roofs, and we can talk through which fits your house and budget.

How long does a replacement take?

It depends on the size and pitch of the roof. We'll tell you how long yours will take when we give you the estimate.

What if it rains during the install?

We only tear off as much as we can cover again that same day, and we watch the forecast before we start.

My roof is leaking right now. What do I do?

Call (912) 852-5526. We can tarp the roof to stop the water, then come back to repair it properly.
